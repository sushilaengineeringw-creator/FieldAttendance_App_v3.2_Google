How paying works

  1. Choose a plan and period inside the app (Company tab) or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay by UPI to the UPI ID shown in the app or on our home page. Write your Company ID in the payment note.
  3. We check that the money has reached us, then switch your plan on. This is done by a person, so it can take a few hours on a working day. We do not switch a plan on before payment is verified.
  4. You get a reminder in the app 7, 3 and 1 day before your plan ends. Renewing before the end date adds to your remaining time.
Please note. The Android app downloaded from Google Play does not take payments inside the app. Plans are bought on this website or by WhatsApp, and then switch on in your account.

What happens when a plan ends

Staff can no longer mark attendance, and reports and attendance lists stay locked until you renew. Your data is not deleted. It is available again as soon as the plan is renewed. You may ask us to delete everything at any time on the delete account page.
