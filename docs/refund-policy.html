Refund policy

Short and plain. You can try everything free for 10 days before you pay anything, so refunds are limited.

Free trial

New companies get 10 days free for up to 5 staff. No payment is taken during the trial.

Paid plans

  • Paid by mistake or twice: if you paid twice for the same plan, or paid an amount that was not for a plan, we refund the extra amount in full.
  • Payment received but plan not switched on: we switch it on after verifying the payment. If we cannot, we refund the full amount.
  • Service did not work for you: write to us within 7 days of the first payment, tell us what went wrong, and give us a chance to fix it. If we cannot fix it, we refund the unused part of the plan.
  • Changed your mind later: plans are for a fixed period and are not refunded after the first 7 days, but you can keep using the plan until it ends.

How to ask

Email sales.sushilaengineering@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp (+91 92843 58304) with your Company ID, the payment date and the payment reference. Approved refunds go back to the same UPI account or bank account the payment came from, normally within 7 working days.
