User guide

Everything you need to start in about 10 minutes. Choose your role below.

1. Install the app

Android phone

Install Field Attendance from Google Play when it is listed there, or use the download link on our home page and install the file. If the phone asks, allow installs from your browser for this one time.

iPhone

Open the app link given by your company in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app. (An App Store version is not available yet.)

2. Administrator: set up your company

  1. Open the app, choose Register your company, and enter company name, your name, mobile number and an administrator password. Your Company ID is shown when registration finishes. Write it down; every staff member needs it.
  2. Sign in with the Company ID, user ADMIN and your password.
  3. Open Employees and add each person: name, mobile, designation and site. The app creates an employee ID and a first PIN for each person. Give them to the person privately.
  4. For a fixed site, set the site location and radius for the person. When it is set, the person can mark attendance only inside that radius. Leave it empty for people who work at changing places.
  5. Open Dashboard to see who is present, absent and where, with check-in photos.
The free trial is 10 days for up to 5 staff. See Plans when you are ready to continue.

3. Employee: mark attendance

  1. Sign in with the Company ID, your employee ID and the PIN your administrator gave you.
  2. Check in: stand at your work place, tap LOGIN (MARK ATTENDANCE), allow camera and location, take a clear photo of your face, and confirm. Wait for the green confirmation.
  3. Check out: tap LOGOUT. Only your location is taken, no photo.
  4. If you forget to check out, the system closes your day automatically 12 hours after check-in so you can check in again the next day.
Tips for a good reading. Switch on Location (GPS) and set it to precise or high accuracy. Wait a few seconds outdoors if accuracy is poor. Do not use a fake-location app.

4. Manager

A manager can see the dashboard, attendance lists and reports for the company. Managers cannot delete the company or change plans. A manager counts as staff in your plan limit.

5. Reports

Open Reports and tap the Excel report you need: Monthly P/L (present and leave), Hours, Location, Complete, or an employee-wise report. The files show every day for every person with times, places and photo links. On Android and iPhone you can save or share the file straight from the app.

6. Passwords, sign-out and account

  • Change administrator password: Company tab, Change my password. A confirmation window appears before it is changed.
  • Administrator forgot password: on the sign-in screen tap Administrator forgot password?, enter the Company ID and the registered mobile number, and send the request. We check it and set a new password for you; you can then change it yourself. You can also contact us on WhatsApp or email.
  • Employee forgot PIN: the administrator opens Employees and uses Reset PIN.
  • Sign out: in the Company tab; it asks you to confirm first.
  • Delete company and all data: Company tab, Delete company account and data, after a confirmation window. See Delete account.

7. Questions and problems

"You are ... m away from your assigned site"

The administrator has set a site for you and you are outside its radius. Go to the site and try again, or ask the administrator to correct the site location or radius.

"Trial ended" or "plan expired"

Staff cannot mark attendance until the plan is renewed. Your data is safe. The administrator can renew with Buy / renew plan in the app or from Plans.

The camera or location does not work

Open the phone Settings, find Field Attendance (or Safari on iPhone), and allow Camera and Location. Switch on GPS and try again.

I checked in but the phone lost internet

Wait for a connection and tap the button again once. The system recognises a repeated request and will not record a second check-in.

Is my data private?

Each company can see only its own data. Photos and locations are used only for attendance. Read the Privacy Policy.
