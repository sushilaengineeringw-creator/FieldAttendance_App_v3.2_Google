Company administrators: delete inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in as ADMIN.
  2. Go to the Company tab.
  3. Tap Delete company account and data and enter your administrator password.

Attendance is switched off for the whole company immediately. The company's sheet, login photos and reports are then permanently deleted, normally within 30 days. Until then you can tap Cancel deletion request in the same place. Please download any monthly reports you need first.

Cannot open the app?

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from the email address (or mobile number) you registered with, and include your Company ID and company name. We will confirm the request with you and then delete the company's data within 30 days.

Employees

Employee accounts are created by the employer. To have your records corrected or deleted, ask your company administrator, or email us at the address above with your company name, Company ID and Employee ID, and we will take it up with the company.

What is deleted, what is kept

Deleted: the company's attendance sheet, employee list, login photos, saved reports, the administrator's name, mobile number, email and address, and the sign-in password. Kept: a minimal record that the company existed and was deleted (company ID, date), and anything we must keep by law.
