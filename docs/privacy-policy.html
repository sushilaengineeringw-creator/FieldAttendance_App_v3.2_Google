Field Attendance

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Applies to the Field Attendance mobile app ("the App")

This App is provided by Sushila Engineering Works, Pimprala, Plot No 17, Veer Savarkar Nagar, Jalgaon, Maharashtra, India ("we", "us"). It lets a company record when its employees start and finish work, with a photo and a location. This policy explains what is collected, why, who can see it and how to have it deleted.

In short: the App records an employee's photo and GPS location only when the employee taps Login (photo + location) or Logout (location only). It does not track location in the background, has no advertising, and we do not sell personal data.

1. Who is responsible for the data

The company that registered in the App (the employer) decides to use attendance records and is responsible for informing its employees and having a lawful basis for the records. We process the data on the company's behalf to run the App. If you are an employee and have a question about your records, please ask your employer first.

3. Camera and location permissions

The App asks for Camera (to take the login photo) and Location while using the app (to record the position when you tap Login or Logout). You can withdraw these permissions in your phone settings, but you will then not be able to mark attendance.

4. Who can see the data

  • The employee can see their own history.
  • The company administrator can see all employees of that company; a manager can see only the employees assigned to that manager.
  • Companies cannot see each other's data.
  • We (the provider) can access the stored data for support, security and to delete it on request.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers.

5. Where it is stored and who processes it

Records are stored in Google Sheets and Google Drive (Google LLC) in an account controlled by us, with a separate spreadsheet and folder for every company. Google acts as our hosting provider. Data travels between the App and our server over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Photo links: login photos are stored in a Drive folder that is not listed or searchable. A photo can be opened only by someone who has its exact link. These links appear only inside Excel reports that the company administrator downloads; keep those reports private.

Reports are created on the administrator's phone and saved or shared only where the administrator chooses.

6. How long we keep it

We keep a company's records while its account is active. When a company asks for deletion (see section 7) its attendance records, photos and reports are permanently deleted, normally within 30 days. Records may be kept longer only where the law requires it.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Company administrators can delete the whole company account and all its data inside the App: Company tab → Delete company account and data. You can also request it by email, see how to delete your account and data.

Employees can ask their employer to correct or delete their records, or contact us at the address below; we will pass the request to the company or act on it as the law requires. You may also ask what data is held about you.

8. Security

Passwords and PINs are stored as salted hashes, every request is checked on the server for the user's role and company, repeated wrong sign-ins lock the account for a short time, and access is limited to what each role needs. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your PIN private and tell your administrator if you lose your phone.

9. Children

The App is for working adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell company administrators in the App or by email.
