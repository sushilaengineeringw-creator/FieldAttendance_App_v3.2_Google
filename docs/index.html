Set up in a few minutes

  1. Install the app and register your company

    Enter your company name, your name, a mobile number and a password. The app gives you a Company ID.

  2. Add your staff

    The app makes an ID and a PIN for each person. Share them on WhatsApp.

  3. Staff check in and out

    At check-in they take a selfie and the app records the GPS location. At check-out it records the location only.

  4. Download the monthly register

    One Excel file with present, leave and weekly off for every person, hours worked, and a location report with photo links.

What you get

Selfie at check-in
A front-camera photo is taken at check-in, so you can see who started work.
GPS location
Latitude, longitude and a map link for every check-in and check-out. Location is recorded only when staff tap the button, never in the background.
Work site and radius
Set a site and a distance for each person. Check-ins away from the site are flagged for you.
Excel register
P, L, WO and MC codes with Sundays marked, daily hours and totals for every person.
Managers
A manager sees only their own team.
Your data stays separate
Each company's records are kept apart. You can delete your company and all its data from inside the app.

Questions

What does the free trial include?
10 days, for up to 5 staff, with every feature. There is one free trial for each mobile number.
Do my staff need a Google account?
No. They sign in with the Company ID, their own ID and a PIN.
Does it work on iPhone?
Not yet. The app is for Android phones.
Does it need internet?
Yes. Check-in and check-out need internet and GPS.
Where is my data kept?
Records, photos and reports are stored in Google Drive and Google Sheets under our account, in a separate sheet and folders for each company. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
Can I stop paying?
Yes. Do not renew. When a plan ends, attendance and reports switch off, and your records stay until you delete your company inside the app.
How do I add more staff later?
Pick a bigger plan on this page, pay the amount, and send us the screenshot. We switch you to the new plan.
Is the app on Google Play?
We are preparing the Google Play version. Until then, install the app from this page.
